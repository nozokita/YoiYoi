# YoiYoi Terms & Privacy (MVP summary)

This app is for logging alcohol intake and visualizing goals. **It is not medical advice or diagnosis.** Consult a qualified professional for health decisions.

## Feed

Content you share may be visible to others. Inappropriate use may be reported; we may take moderation steps including blocks.

## Data

We may store account and usage data on your device and with services such as Firebase. See the App Store listing and privacy policy for details.

Tap “Agree and continue” only if you understand and accept the above.
